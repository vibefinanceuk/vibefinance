# 0247 — A ring is for a share

**Status: built.** A donut chart, and a palette that survives a dark
surface.

---

## Where a ring is right, and where it is not

**Every in-flight invoice is at exactly one stage.** *Where things are*
is a whole being divided, and the reader's question — *"how much of my
work is stuck in Approval"* — is a proportion.

**Ageing stays bars.** Its buckets have an order and a ring destroys
it: *under a day* and *over thirty days* are not two slices of a pie,
they are two ends of a line.

**That distinction is the whole decision.** A donut used for anything
ordered or compared is decoration wearing a chart's clothes.

### Two details worth keeping

**The total sits in the middle**, because a ring says the shape and a
reader still wants the number — without it somebody adds up the legend.

**Six segments fold to five and a rest**, since a ring of nine slices is
a colour-matching exercise and the palette has five (decision 0242).

---

## A colour on black needs more than the same colour on white

The chart palette was muted in both moods, and on a dark ground it read
as grey.

The night values are saturated now — and **still refuse a verdict**,
which is what keeps them apart from `--text-warning` and
`--text-success`. A five-category chart borrowing those would say a
category was a problem because it happened to be third (decision 0242).

---

## What is not built

- **No hover, no tooltip.** A segment is a colour and a legend, and
  nothing tells you which is which on a touch screen except reading.
- **No animation**, which the reference has and which is not
  information.
- **Still no sparklines**, because there is still no history — the same
  gap decisions 0240 and 0242 recorded.
