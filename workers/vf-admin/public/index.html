Invitations

An email with a link and a 6-digit code. The person chooses their own password, and is given every environment of the customer. It works once, for 72 hours; five wrong codes spend it.

Partners

System integrators who build connectors in a sandbox of their own, for the customers linked to them here. Their sandbox is an ordinary customer: provision its environment as for any customer. Its people are invited by email once it has one: an invitation lets them sign in, and its first administrator needs a user in the sandbox, as any new customer's does; they then add and invite colleagues from Access.
